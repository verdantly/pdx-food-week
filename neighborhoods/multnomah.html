Fried Chicken Week 2026

75-Year Mole Fried Chicken

🍗

Some call it magic, we call it generations of love. Our crispy, made-to-order 100% gluten-free fried chicken is smothered in our family’s 75-year-old mole recipe, crafted with roasted chiles, warm spices, seeds, nuts, and just a touch of chocolate. Every bite is rich, smoky, savory, and unforgettable. Built on immigrant roots, salvadoran tradition, and the belief that great food brings people together. That’s the salvi magic.
