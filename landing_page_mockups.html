Option 1: Dark Mode

This option makes the entire landing page use the brand's primary Ink color (#1A1208) as its background. The text is rendered in Cream (#FBF6EF). Cards use subtle translucent boundaries that light up on hover, creating a premium dark mode dashboard feel.

  • Strongest brand presence: Establishes the Ink tone immediately.
  • Polished dark aesthetic: Subtle card borders avoid harsh lines.
  • Color Pop: The individual colorful explore buttons draw eyes instantly.
