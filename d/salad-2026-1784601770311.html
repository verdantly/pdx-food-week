Bethany’s Table

Scallop Salad

meat Gluten-Free Family Friendly Takeout Available

Bethany’s Table is serving up a decadent scallop salad, their creative take on the classic Mexican Cobb. Featuring seared sea scallops, fresh tomatoes, avocado, red onion, crispy bacon, and crunchy tortilla strips, it’s hearty, flavorful, and a perfect example of a truly satisfying “big salad”! The scallop salad will be available starting this week all the way through August for $29.
