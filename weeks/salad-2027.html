What is Portland Salad Week 2027?

Portland Salad Week, organized by Bridgetown Bites, celebrates the bounty of the Pacific Northwest summer with farm-fresh produce, grain bowls, and crisp artisan salads.

As the event approaches, this page and the PDX Food Week interactive web app will feature the complete directory of participating restaurants, dishes, addresses, hours, and customized filters.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Portland Salad Week 2027?

Portland Salad Week 2027 is anticipated for late July 2027 across Portland farm-to-table spots and cafes.

Who presents Portland Salad Week?

Salad Week is founded and organized by Bridgetown Bites (bridgetownbites.com).
