What is Portland Fried Chicken Week 2027?

Portland Fried Chicken Week, organized by The Actual Portland, highlights Southern, Korean, Nashville hot, and gluten-free crispy fried chicken specialties across the city for $10.

As the event approaches, this page and the PDX Food Week interactive web app will feature the complete directory of participating restaurants, dishes, addresses, hours, and customized filters.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Portland Fried Chicken Week 2027?

Portland Fried Chicken Week 2027 is expected in mid-September 2027 across Portland restaurants, food carts, and bars.

Who presents Portland Fried Chicken Week?

Fried Chicken Week is organized by The Actual Portland (theactualportland.com).
