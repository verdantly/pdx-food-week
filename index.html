Your unofficial Portland food week companion

Start by picking a food week and browsing through all the options, save the dishes you want to try, and share your favorites with others.
Portland's food weeks are better with friends!

How It Works

01

Explore specials

Browse all the food week specials and filter by location, dietary options, then save your favorites. Use our fun Tinder-style swipe feature to help you choose!

02

Share with friends

Share your must-try dishes with friends and family and see what places everyone has in common.

03

Plan your visits

Build walking or driving itineraries to make it easy to get to all the places you want to try.
