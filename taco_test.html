Taco Locations.

Below is a map showcasing all the participating businesses for PDX Taco Week. Each pin marks a location where you can enjoy special taco creations. Scroll further down to find a detailed list of each business and the unique tacos they’re serving. (Some places will be selling 1 taco for 5$ or 2 tacos for the same price.) Taco Week is from June 1st - 7th 2026.
